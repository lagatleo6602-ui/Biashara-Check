How to install

  1. Open Biashara Check in Chrome (the address you use to log in).
  2. Tap Install app at the top of the page, or open Chrome's menu ⋮ and choose Install app (on some phones: Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. The Biashara Check icon appears on your home screen and in your apps.
  4. Open it from the icon and log in once. You stay logged in for 12 hours at a time.
  1. Open Biashara Check in Safari (installing only works from Safari on iPhone and iPad).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from the new icon on your home screen and log in.
  1. Open Biashara Check in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click Install app at the top of the page, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow).
  3. Click Install. Biashara Check opens in its own window and gets a Start-menu or Dock icon.

Good to know

Does it cost anything?
No. Installing is free and uses very little space (well under 1 MB).
Does it work without internet?
It opens without a connection and shows a short message, but your figures, risk check and reports need the internet, because they're kept safely on the server, not on the phone.
How does it update?
By itself: every time you open it with a connection you get the latest version.
Is my data on the phone?
No. Figures are never stored on the device, so a lost phone doesn't expose them. Log out on shared phones.
How do I remove it?
Like any app: press and hold the icon and choose Uninstall or Remove. Your account and figures stay safe on the server.
I don't see "Install app"
Use Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone, or Chrome or Edge on a computer. The address must start with https:// (or be this computer's own 127.0.0.1). If you've already installed it, the option disappears: open it from the icon.
